ABOUT
Shivane Augustus (Theverapperuma) is a Toronto based entrepreneur and director with an active portfolio across media, hospitality, travel and marketing. He currently serves as Director of Creative Touch Media Inc. and Premium Global Expeditions Inc. in Canada, and holds marketing leadership roles with Lake Villas Premium in Kandy, Sri Lanka, and Hershley Group Pvt Ltd. Alongside his operating roles, he is an active angel investor, backing early-stage businesses where his international network and marketing expertise can accelerate growth.

Born and raised in Colombo, Sri Lanka, and now based in Toronto, Canada,
Shivane brings a cross-market perspective shaped by experience building brands in both the Canadian and South Asian business landscapes.

EARLY LIFE / EDUCATION
Shivane was educated at Stafford International School in Colombo 7, Sri Lanka, before relocating to Canada to continue his studies at Humber Polytechnic. He also studied at the University of London, in the United Kingdom. This international academic foundation spanning Sri Lanka, Canada, and the UK underpins the cross-border perspective he brings to every business he's involved in today.
